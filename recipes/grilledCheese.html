Grilled Cheese

Description

To make a classic grilled cheese, cook a buttered bread and cheese sandwich in a skillet over medium-low heat for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and melted.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Cheese
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Butter the bread
  2. Assemble the sandwich
  3. Cook low and slow
  4. Flip and melt
