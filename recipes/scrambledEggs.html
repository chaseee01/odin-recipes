Scrambled Egg

Description

Perfect scrambled eggs are light, fluffy, and tender, featuring a delicate balance of rich, buttery flavor and a savory finish. They are gently cooked to form soft, pillow-like curds that remain slightly moist and glossy, ensuring they are never dry or rubbery. This classic breakfast staple is incredibly versatile, making it the perfect foundation for a quick morning meal or a comforting brunch.

Ingredients

  • 3 to 4 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • A pinch of salt and black pepper
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons milk or water

Steps

  1. Beat the eggs
  2. Heat the pan
  3. Cook the eggs
  4. Scramble and fold
  5. Remove from heat
  6. Serve
